Bear Lake what an amazing place

Bear Lake has tones of visitors year round for various reasons, some being fro the lakes turquoise water, summer water recreation, camping, and for some "The Bear Lake monster". Bear Lake's light blue waters attracts visitors from all around the country, although over the last few decades Bear Lake has had a threat to it's bright color due to sediment, and algae making the water more murky.
