Rock climbing slang can be confusing at first byt when you get it down you will use the word beta more than any other word

Some slang words may be weird at first but after you get tem down there more convinent then anythingelse. A good slang word can be beta. The word beta in climbing means the intended way to do the climb, this means if you need help to learn how to do the climb you can just ask fro the beta. The next most important slang word in climbing has to be the word flash. This word DOES NOT mean take you clothes off for the climb, but rather means a climb you can do first try. The name of holds are alos important, and most are very easy to remember. The first hold is called a jug and it is teh most loved hold in climbing becasue you are able to fit your whole hand into the hold for good grip. Nest is called a crimp, the crimp it a hold that you hold wiht only your fingers, and they can suck to hold unless you like to do them. next up is a pinch, the pinch is self explanetory, you just pinch the hold. A slopper is also a challenging hold becasue there is no right way to hold it becasue it is rust a round plastic rock. They are still fun to climb though; Lastly there is a pocket, the pokcet is a hold that is a hole that you can fit up to 2 or 3 fingers into. What grades to start at when beginning and what they mean.

The grading system for rock climbing can be confusing for beginners but in reality it is very simple. The lowest grade possiable is a v0. This is the easyest climb to do in any gym, the highest most gyms go to is a v13. Outside climbing there are some that go to v17 which only 5 to 12 people in the world can do! It can take time to go up in climbs but with pracice or even joining a team, progress can be almost instant.

  • easy climbs v0-v3
  • medium climbs v3-v6
  • hard climbs v6-v10
  • professional climbs v10+

The trick to doing higher grades like a v1 to v3 is doing a climb just out of your flash grade

By doing a climb right abouve your skill level you are constantly pushing yourself to try harder on almost all climbs. Just remember to take a break every so often so you don't injure yourself. To not injure yourself while climbing it is important to always stretch before climbing. This is so that you are more flexible to be able to reach holds just out of reach.
